## Known behaviour worth knowing
- Cart on pro.meca.se is **per vehicle**; staging requires that vehicle's cart to be empty (else the booking is blocked with a message). An expired approval leaves the cart filled for the owner to clear (spec); Cancel clears it.
- Only parts under "Kopplade reservdelar" are candidates; discontinued ("utgått") and replaced ("Ersätts av") items are excluded; ProMeister is hard-coded as forbidden; fluids and oils are never ordered (some real parts are over-blocked, e.g. "Fettnippel", "Smörjnippel", English "Oil pump" — order those by hand).
- Axle-specific parts (belägg, bromsskiva, stötdämpare, …) without an axle in the booking line are blocked, not guessed.
- Ceiling 10,000 SEK is compared against the higher of the cart total and the checkout-page total, at staging, at approval and inside `checkout()` before the final click.
- `logs/agent.log` (uploaded as a 14-day workflow artifact) can contain full booking details — scrub before trusting it with more than a small workshop's data.

## Package 2 — the parts (2026-10-22)
Built and reviewed, all local until the owner pushes (design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-package2-parts-design.md`, with a Status note listing the deviations). `parts_rules.yaml` keeps `mode: approve`; its new keys are commented examples only.
- **Rules as data** (`rules.py`): optional `brand_prefer_by_part`, `prefer_in_stock` (true), `expensive_ratio` (1.5), `expensive_min_diff_sek` (100), `max_alternatives` (1, 0-3). Strict validation; a non-finite ratio and duplicate part keys are rejected.
- **Stock-aware choice and alternatives** (`parts_matcher.choose_with_alternatives`): in `approve` mode an out-of-stock pick is replaced by a picker-confirmed in-stock part (preferred brands first, then all in-stock); the line says `Bytt från <titel> (slut)`. A line can get an alternative ("slut" or "dyr", `sparar N SEK/st`) under the usual filters. At most one alternative is ever offered, whatever `max_alternatives` is. `auto` mode never replaces or offers alternatives.
- **Telegram:** Godkänn/Avbryt plus one `Byt <del>` row per flagged line (`swap:<booking_id>:<index>`). A text over 4000 characters drops the alternative block and swap rows.
- **Swap** (`approve._swap`, `parts_order.restage_with_pins`): owner only, record `pending`, own message id, at most 2 per booking, 240 s budget, clears the cart, restages with the pin, new approval message and message id, old buttons cleared, never orders; each swap refreshes the 48 h window; failure texts for an empty, partly filled or full cart.
- **Immediate verification** (`parts_order.verify_now`): after the Beställd message (sent first, unchanged) the order is looked up by plate in "Skickade Beställningar" (3 reads, 10 s apart, 90 s budget; not after approve run start + 270 s or past the staging deadline in auto) and a match gets its own `Beställning verifierad` message. The daily checker stays the fallback. Residual risk: a same-plate earlier row when the reference is UNVERIFIED.
- **Owner to check at the first live use:** a swap end to end on a real cart (cart clear, re-add, new unit prices); that the 4-minute swap fits the approve step; whether verification finds the real order within 90 s.
- Parked: `max_alternatives` 2-3 act as 1; exact duplicate YAML keys are not detected; an undelivered verified message does not turn the run red; the 48 h window restarts on every swap.
